00 / Approach

Understand first, then build

I start by understanding how an organisation actually works: who does what, with which systems, and where the work gets stuck. Only then do I build — developing with coding agents under explicit architecture, review and verification, and leaving the team able to run what it receives. I do this work through Tensoria Consulting.

Since 2020 I have been the technology partner of an accounting practice: first its office infrastructure, then a map of how information moves through the firm, then the internal tool it runs on and a client portal now in pilot.

At AlterMundi, a community-networks organisation, I ran the infrastructure and architected SAI, a wildfire early-detection pilot whose edge nodes worked unattended for months.
